What is a poster pair?
Two colors plus ink and ground, taken from one album cover. One app gets one pair, forever.
Why is the jitter irregular?
Because perfectly-eased motion is off-brand. Live things blink like a VU meter, not a metronome.
Where do the chart colors come from?
Six validated slots per pair per theme. A machine checked them for colorblind separation and contrast. Do not freestyle.
